## English summary

The source materials are guides published by the Personal Information Protection
Commission (PIPC) of the Republic of Korea and consultation cases from the
Personal Information Portal (privacy.go.kr). The original copyright notice on
the source materials reads, in Korean: *"Unauthorized reproduction is
prohibited; when processing or citing, please indicate the source — Personal
Information Protection Commission."*

When using, citing, or redistributing this dataset, you must attribute the
source materials and this dataset's URL above. Semantic chunking, the
`chunk_context` field (Anthropic Contextual Retrieval, 2024), and the
standardized schema are derivative works by this dataset's contributor and are
released under the same conditions. We will promptly remove or amend any part
of this dataset upon request from the original rightsholder.
